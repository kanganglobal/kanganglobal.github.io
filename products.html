What is Kangen Water?

Kangen Water is a revolutionary type of ionized water designed to improve health and well-being. Created by Enagic, a global leader in water filtration and ionization technology, it is purified, alkaline, and antioxidant-rich.

"Kangen" translates to "return to origin" in Japanese, symbolizing the restoration of the body to its natural, balanced state.

Key Features of Production

Alkalinity

Adjustable pH (8.5-9.5) to help neutralize body acidity and support overall health.

Antioxidant Rich

Infused with molecular hydrogen to combat free radicals and reduce oxidative stress.

Micro-Clustering

Smaller water clusters for superior hydration and efficient cellular absorption.
