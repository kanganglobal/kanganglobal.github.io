The Science Behind Kangen Water
Kangen water is produced using state-of-the-art water ionization machines by Enagic, which are certified medical-grade devices. These machines utilize a process called electrolysis to separate water into two streams: alkaline water and acidic water. During this process, water is filtered to remove impurities such as chlorine, heavy metals, and other contaminants, ensuring that only the purest and healthiest water reaches your glass.

Rich in Antioxidants:
Kangen water is infused with molecular hydrogen, one of the most powerful antioxidants known. This antioxidant combats free radicals—unstable molecules that cause oxidative stress in the body. Micro-Clustering Technology:
Ordinary water molecules cluster in groups of 12-16, but Kangen water breaks these clusters into smaller groups of 5-6 molecules. This process, known as micro-clustering, enhances the water’s ability to penetrate cells, improving hydration, nutrient absorption, and overall cellular health.
