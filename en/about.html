About Enagic & Kangen Water

Explore our history, mission, and values.

Enagic Company Profile

Enagic is a global health equipment manufacturer based in Okinawa, Japan, specializing in high-quality water ionization systems. Founded in 1974, with over 45 years of history, it has grown to become one of the world's leading manufacturers of alkaline ionizers.

Our History

Enagic was founded by Hironari Oshiro in 1974 in Okinawa, Japan. Initially, the company focused on providing industrial-grade water processing equipment for Japanese hospitals and clinics.

In 1988, Enagic began developing home water ionizers, aiming to bring medical-grade water technology into ordinary households.

In 2003, Enagic started its international expansion, first entering the North American market, then expanding to Asia, Europe, and other regions. Today, Enagic has 25 offices worldwide, with products sold in over 80 countries.

Enagic's mission is to help people achieve a healthier lifestyle by providing high-quality Kangen Water technology. We are committed to:

What is Kangan Water?

Origin of Kangen Water

"Kangen" is a Japanese term meaning "Return to Origin". Kangen Water refers to alkaline electrolyzed water produced by Enagic's machines, possessing unique physical and chemical properties.

Enagic founder Hironari Oshiro was inspired by the properties of drinking water in Japanese longevity villages while researching the impact of water quality on health, leading to the development of technology capable of producing water with similar characteristics.

Three Properties of Kangen Water

  • Alkaline pH

    pH ranges from 8.5-9.5, helping to balance the body's acidity.

  • Negative ORP (Antioxidant)

    Has a negative Oxidation-Reduction Potential (ORP), indicating antioxidant properties.

  • Micro-Clustering

    Water molecules exist in smaller clusters, which may aid in better hydration.

Kangen Water Philosophy

Kangen Water is not just a product, but a lifestyle philosophy emphasizing a return to nature, bodily balance, and environmental friendliness. This philosophy resonates with the traditional Japanese philosophy of harmony and balance.
