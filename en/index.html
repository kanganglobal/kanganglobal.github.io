Unique Advantages of Kangen Water

Antioxidant Properties

Kangen Water has a negative Oxidation-Reduction Potential (ORP), meaning it acts as an antioxidant to help neutralize free radicals in the body.

  • Selectively neutralizes harmful free radicals
  • Does not interfere with beneficial oxidative reactions
  • May help reduce oxidative stress

Multi-Functional Water System

One Enagic machine can produce various types of water with different pH levels to meet diverse household needs.

  • Alkaline Drinking Water (pH 8.5-9.5)
  • Strong Acidic Water for Sanitizing (pH 2.5)
  • Beauty Water (pH 5.5-6.0)
  • Neutral Water for Meds & Baby Formula
